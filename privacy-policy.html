Privacy Policy

InvoSee — internal accounts-payable application · Last updated: November 2026

1. What this application is

InvoSee is an internal accounts-payable tool. The Application and all data processed through it are the property of Fauji Foods Limited, a company incorporated in Pakistan, and FonGrow Pvt Limited ("FonGrow", together with Fauji Foods Limited, the "Owners"). It is used by Fauji Foods Limited and its approved business units to extract, review, and approve supplier invoices, and to post approved invoices into each business's own connected accounting system, including QuickBooks Online. It is not a public product — it has no public sign-up, and access is limited to employees granted login credentials by an administrator.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy describes how InvoSee accesses, uses, and stores data obtained through its integration with the QuickBooks Online Accounting API (via Intuit's OAuth 2.0 authorization flow).

3. QuickBooks data we access

When a company administrator connects a QuickBooks Online company to Invoice Register, the application reads:

  • Vendors — to match a supplier invoice to the correct QuickBooks vendor record.
  • Chart of Accounts — to let a reviewer categorize an invoice line against the company's own expense accounts.
  • Products and Services (Items) — to let a reviewer post an invoice line as a specific item where applicable.

The application writes:

  • Bills — created in QuickBooks Online once a supplier invoice has been reviewed and approved inside Invoice Register.

Invoice Register does not access QuickBooks Customers, Sales/AR Invoices, Payments, Payroll, or banking/bank-feed data.

4. How we use this data

QuickBooks vendor, account, and item data is used solely to let a reviewer correctly categorize a supplier invoice before it is posted. Invoice data entered or extracted within Invoice Register (vendor name, invoice number, date, line items, amounts) is sent to QuickBooks Online only as part of creating the corresponding Bill record in the company the invoice belongs to.

5. How we store and protect data

  • OAuth 2.0 access and refresh tokens issued by Intuit are encrypted at rest in our database and are never exposed to end users or stored in plain text.
  • Each connected business's own Intuit app credentials (client ID and secret) are likewise encrypted at rest.
  • All communication with Intuit's servers occurs over HTTPS/TLS.
  • Access to Invoice Register itself is restricted to authenticated employees with role-based permissions (reviewer, approver, pusher, admin) scoped to their own business.
  • The application is hosted on private infrastructure that is not exposed to the public internet.

6. Data sharing and ownership

We do not sell, rent, or share QuickBooks data with any third party. Data obtained through the QuickBooks Online API is used exclusively within Invoice Register for the purpose described above. All data processed through the Application remains the property of Fauji Foods Limited and FonGrow Pvt Limited, or of the business unit the data belongs to, as applicable.

7. Data retention and disconnecting

QuickBooks OAuth tokens are retained only for as long as a company's QuickBooks connection remains active. A company administrator may revoke Invoice Register's access at any time from within QuickBooks Online's own "Connected Apps" settings; doing so immediately invalidates the stored tokens.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in how Invoice Register integrates with QuickBooks Online. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
